# Ivi — Feature List

## MVP

1. **Diet-plan builder** — meal-by-meal composition, quantity per item, per-day structure
2. **Food database** — exchange table, national composition table and USDA data, Greek-localized
3. **Energy requirement calculation** — Harris-Benedict BMR, activity multiplier, weight-loss target, per-kg coefficients, manual override
4. **Macronutrient distribution** — carb/protein/fat percentage split
5. **Real-time daily progress bars** — energy and macros updating as foods are added
6. **Client records** — CRUD, card and list views, tags, goals, contact details
7. **Anthropometric measurements** — value history and change charts
8. **PDF export / print of the diet** — carrying the practice's own logo
9. **Cloud, browser-based** — no installation; desktop, tablet, mobile
10. **Session journal** — free-text record per consultation

## Phase 1

11. **Template meals** — save a meal composition, insert it by name
12. **Template diet plans** — save whole plans, copy to any client
13. **Copy/paste meal or entire day** — plus drag-and-drop reordering
14. **Automatic plan generation** — complete personalized plan in under a minute
15. **Questionnaires** — validated instruments plus a custom questionnaire builder
16. **Pre-visit questionnaire sharing** — emailed link the client completes before arriving
17. **Completed-answers summary view**
18. **Calendar** — appointments, durations, notes, non-client events
19. **Two-way Google Calendar sync**
20. **Auto-generated shopping list** — on the plan PDF
21. **Plan analysis** — per-day energy and macros, food statistics, weekly averages
22. **Extended measurements** — skinfolds, circumferences, blood markers with range flags, bulk entry, printable results with charts
23. **Per-client file storage** — lab results, documents, spreadsheets
24. **Custom foods** — add a food with its energy, composition and micronutrients
25. **Favorite foods** — flag foods across any table, collected into one list
26. **Custom print templates** — colors, borders, fonts, category color-coding, hide analysis or quantities, rename days and meal times, multiple templates selectable per client

## Phase 2

27. **Client mobile app** — food logging with photo and text, exercise logging, water tracking, log history, view plans and notes, push notifications
28. **Real-time micronutrient tracking** — selectable micronutrients with targets and ceilings, updating live as foods are added
29. **DRI display** — percentage of reference intake, hover for absolute values
30. **Consolidated client messaging** — one professional channel replacing scattered messaging apps and email
31. **Recipes** — ingredients, method, use inside plans, printable, with generated nutrition label
32. **Body-composition analyzer import** — device-agnostic CSV ingest with mapping layer
33. **Business statistics** — revenue by period, cancellations, appointments held, projected revenue, client totals, period comparisons
34. **Selective history export to PDF** — choose which sections to share with another professional
35. **Saved plan notes** — reusable across plans
36. **Custom food naming within a plan** — rename entries, attach preparation instructions
37. **Dashboard** — client birthdays, to-do list, the day's appointments, quick actions

## Phase 3

38. **Clinical conditions module** — per-condition guidance, printable patient instructions, supplement suggestions, background theory, diagnostic tools
39. **Scientific calculators** — child BMI classification, triglyceride-glucose index, dietary fiber requirement, chronic kidney disease staging
40. **Practitioner network** — exchange plans, recipes and foods, with discussion and save-to-templates
41. **Micronutrient export to CSV** — per-day values, DRI percentages, tolerable upper intake levels
42. **Feature request and voting board**
43. **Public changelog**
44. **In-app support staffed by practising dietitians** — alongside phone and email
45. **Onboarding** — step-by-step training videos plus 1:1 sessions on request
46. **Self-service demo booking**
